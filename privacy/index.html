Privacy Policy

DevDeyaa Systems, run by Deyaa Ahmed, Giza, Egypt. Last updated 5 November 2026.

DevDeyaa Systems builds websites and software for businesses, mainly dental clinics in Cairo and Giza. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it for us, and how to have it deleted. It covers this website, the websites we build and host under devdeyaa.com, and the messages we send through the WhatsApp Business Platform from +20 100 959 1202.

What we collect

  • Business contact details. The name, area and phone number a business publishes on its public Google Maps listing, and any public website or social page it lists there. We use these to contact the business about our services.
  • WhatsApp messages and their status. When we message a business on WhatsApp, Meta reports to us whether the message was sent, delivered, read or failed, with timestamps and the message's pricing category. When you write to us, we receive your message, your WhatsApp profile name and your number.
  • Taps on links in our messages. When you tap a button in our WhatsApp message, we record the time, an approximate city and country derived from the network, and the browser's user agent, so we know which business asked to talk to us.
  • The contact form on this site. The name, email address and message you send.
  • Visits to sites we host. Page, time, device type and approximate city and country. We do not use advertising cookies or sell this data.

How we use it

  • To introduce our services to a business and reply to it.
  • To stop messaging anyone who asks us to stop.
  • To measure our own outreach (how many messages arrived, were read or answered).

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) carries our WhatsApp messages.
  • Cloudflare hosts our websites and the database where we keep the records above.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.

Each processes data under its own terms and privacy policy. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

Stopping our messages

Tap "مش مهتمين" (not interested) under our message, reply "stop", or block the number. Any of these stops further messages. We keep your number on a do-not-contact list so we never message it again.

Your data and how to delete it

To see, correct or delete the data we hold about you or your business, send a WhatsApp message to +20 112 009 6771 or use the contact form, with the phone number concerned. We reply and complete the request within 30 days.

After deletion we keep only the phone number on the do-not-contact list, unless you ask us to remove it too.

How long we keep it

Outreach records (messages, statuses and taps) are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted. Contact-form messages are kept while we are in contact with you. Visit records for hosted sites are kept for up to 24 months.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Our records sit in a password-protected dashboard, and messages from Meta are accepted only when signed with our app secret.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
